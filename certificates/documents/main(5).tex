\title[The companion problem: a programme report]
      {The companion problem, from harmonic sums to modular anchors:\\
       a programme report}

\author{}
\date{\today}

\begin{abstract}
This is the narrative report of a three-day campaign (2026-08-03--05) of
the odd-zeta programme on the \emph{companion problem}: closed formulas for
the second solutions of Ap\'ery-like recurrences --- the fifteen sporadic
pairs, and the rows of the Brown--Zudilin cellular family for $\zeta(5)$.
The campaign began as a siege of one identity (the programme's
``bridge''), produced a wall of sharply structured negatives, extracted
from those negatives first a proof-shaped obstruction (curve-blindness),
then an instrument (the nome/anchor calculus), and ended with a proved
theory: an abstract companion inversion theorem; a proof that all fifteen
sporadic operators are exactly rectified by their nomes --- for the six
third-order families via explicit symmetric-square identities with a
uniform closed form for the square root --- and hence a uniform,
all-$n$ Eichler-integral companion formula for every family, including the
seven for which the complete tame harmonic ansatz is (precisely,
finitely) excluded. Along the way: proved modular identifications, a new
uniform weight-one congruence law that survives sixteen held-out primes
per family, a proved annihilation law for a ``formula-less'' family's
summand, a census finding no sixteenth sporadic pair in a defined search
universe, mirror-type signatures of the $\zeta(5)$ operator, one refuted
conjecture (reported with pride), and one small delightful object (the
half-Ap\'ery numbers) whose integrality is honestly trivial and whose
Lucas law sees the class field of $\Q(\sqrt{-6})$. We narrate the
campaign in the order it happened, because the logic of discovery ---
negatives with structure pointing at the missing instrument --- is the
transferable lesson. Every claim carries its evidence label and a pointer
to its technical home; this survey asserts nothing above its source's
grade.
\end{abstract}

\section{The problem, and the discipline}\label{sec:problem}

\subsection{Companions}
An \emph{Ap\'ery-like pair} is a three-term recurrence with two
distinguished solutions: an integral one $A(n)$, given by a binomial sum,
and a \emph{companion} $B(n)$ (normalised $B(0)=0$, $B(1)=1$), whose
arithmetic --- denominators, congruences, limits --- carries the
irrationality-theoretic content. Ap\'ery's $\zeta(3)$ proof is the
archetype: $a_n=\sum_k\bin nk^2\bin{n+k}k^2$, and the companion with
$b_n/a_n\to\zeta(3)$, whose \emph{closed formula} (a binomial sum against
a harmonic weight) is what makes the denominator bound $d_n^3b_n\in\Z$
provable. The fifteen sporadic pairs --- Zagier's six, the six
Almkvist--Zudilin cases, Cooper's three --- generalise the archetype; for
eight of them harmonic companion formulas were known, and for seven
(``the conjectural seven'': $\mathbf B,\mathbf C,\mathbf F,\delta,\zeta,
\eta,s_{18}$) none was known, with mounting evidence of absence.

One floor up sits the Brown--Zudilin cellular family for $\zeta(5)$: a
certified third-order recurrence $\Lbz$ with rows $(Q_n,\hat P_n,P_n)$
and
$I_n=Q_n(2\zeta(5)+4\zeta(3)\zeta(2))-4\hat P_n\zeta(2)-2P_n$. The middle
row has a proved companion formula; the top row's compact formula
\begin{equation}\label{eq:bridge}
P_n\overset{?}{=}\sum_{k,l=0}^nT(n,k,l)\,\omega_5(n,k,l),
\qquad
T=\bin{n+k}{n}\bin nk^2\bin{n+l}{n}\bin nl^2\bin{n+k+l}{n},
\tag{BRIDGE}
\end{equation}
is verified far beyond reasonable doubt and \Open; it is the single
identity on which the programme's sharp low-prime denominator theorem for
$P_n$ (the factor $2^2\cdot3$) currently rests. Sharper proved
denominators are the only known lever on the worthiness exponent of the
$\zeta(5)$ approximations --- which is why one identity gets a siege.

\subsection{The discipline}
The programme's evidence classes --- \Thm/\Proved, \VerifiedR{range},
\ExcludedR{scope}, \Open, and (used once below, with satisfaction)
\Refuted\ --- are load-bearing and never silently upgraded. Two standing
rules were adopted mid-campaign at a referee's insistence and are now
programme law: finite coefficient agreement is never called an identity,
and finite integrality is never called modularity. This survey cites four
technical papers --- \MA\ (\texttt{modular\_anchors}, the theory,
post-referee), \VA\ (\texttt{variational\_anchor}, the jet calculus),
\EA\ (\texttt{expository\_apery}), \HA\ (\texttt{half\_apery}) --- and
the memos and scripts catalogued in Appendix~\ref{sec:provenance}.

\section{Act I: the sieges, and what their failure taught}
\label{sec:sieges}

The campaign opened as an assault on \eqref{eq:bridge} through its
$\varepsilon$-deformation reduction: the bridge follows (in part) from a
single cellwise identity $\sum T\Delta_5=0$, whose symmetrised form had
long been known to lie in the per-row kernel of the cell --- and to sit
\emph{exactly one dimension} outside the span of every proved family of
residue-calculus generators.

Six generator classes were built or completed, each calibrated (every
generator individually certified as an identity) at two unrelated 22-bit
primes:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em,itemsep=1pt]
\item fixed-pole jets, evaluation and derivative towers, and the
anti-diagonal Laurent family (the programme's prior alphabet, re-based);
\item elementary-symmetric pole cancellations on the simple-zero endpoint
ranges and the full numerator multiset (rank $424\to426$);
\item $z$-weighted jets --- valid identities living outside the pure
harmonic ring, requiring an extended ring with polynomial letters
($2030$ generators; rank $1497$);
\item $z^2$-weighted jets and $z$-weighted towers ($2429$ generators;
rank $2714$);
\item nested harmonic-weighted jets, lattice and endpoint, with nested
letters ($258$ generators);
\item and, on the telescoping side, capped order-zero certificates for
the residual (excluded at depths $1$--$3$, three denominator families,
zero nullity at depth 3).
\end{enumerate}
The target remained exactly one dimension outside every span
\ExcludedR{two primes, identical ranks; scripts
\texttt{eps26}--\texttt{eps32}, \texttt{t\_deep\_r}}. Meanwhile the membership fact itself was
stress-tested and held: the per-row kernel rank saturates at exactly
$1673$ out to $n\le75$ \VerifiedR{\texttt{eps33}} --- a per-row
decomposition exists; the constructive alphabet simply cannot reach it.

Two consolidations made the impasse precise. The missing dimension was
extracted as one explicit $231$-monomial identity with exact rational
coefficients (two-prime CRT, matching pivot patterns;
\texttt{eps31}) --- the narrowest formulation of the gap to date. And
capped order-zero telescoping certificates for that residual were
excluded at inflation depths $1$--$3$, three denominator families, with
zero nullity at depth 3 (\texttt{t\_deep\_r}).

\begin{lesson}\label{les:local}
Six independent local mechanisms missing by exactly one dimension is not
bad luck; it is a signature. All the generators were \emph{local}
functionals of one kernel. The missing direction is a global invariant no
local instrument detects. The productive move was not a seventh local
family but the question: \emph{what class of instruments are we actually
using, and what is its reach?}
\end{lesson}

\section{Act II: curve-blindness, and the tool that fell out}
\label{sec:blind}

The reach question has a clean answer for the analytic instruments. A
\emph{curve atom} is a deformation of the cell along a polynomial curve
$T(n+\alpha(\varepsilon),k+\beta(\varepsilon),l+\gamma(\varepsilon))$ ---
the meromorphically honest deformations, each a Brown--Zudilin parametric
integrand at shifted parameters.

\begin{lemma}[coupling; three lines, degree-independent]\label{lem:coupling}
\Proved\ (\VA)\quad
A harmonic monomial of weight $w$ at $\varepsilon$-order $r$ needs
letter-factors with $m_i\ge j_i$, $\sum m_i=r$, $\sum j_i=w$; at top grade
$w=r$ this forces $m_i=j_i$ and $[\varepsilon^j]s_L^j=d_{1,L}^j$. So the
top-graded content of $[\varepsilon^r]$ of \emph{any} polynomial curve,
of any degree, depends only on its first-order direction, through pure
$r$-th powers. Higher curve coefficients feed only lower weights; and for
mixtures, the fifth-symmetric moment slot cannot be steered independently
of the lower moments (polarization is blocked --- both move at the same
order).
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[Veronese saturation]\label{lem:sat}
\Proved\ (exact ranks $6,10,15,21$; \MA)\quad
The direction box $\{-2..2\}^3$ has full-rank Veronese images at degrees
$2$--$5$; the finite computations therefore certify the top grade for
\emph{every} integer direction, hence every polynomial curve.
\end{lemma}

\begin{theorem}[curve-blindness]\label{thm:blind}
(\MA, \VA)\quad The top-graded $\varepsilon^5$ content of any pinned
combination of polynomial-curve atoms has vanishing $P_n$-component ---
unconditionally, by Lemmas \ref{lem:coupling}--\ref{lem:sat}; and on the
stated boxes the entire $\varepsilon^5$ row is confined to
$\Q Q\oplus\Q\hat P$ \ExcludedR{two primes; \texttt{eps41/42}}. The
$\varepsilon^3$ row meanwhile realises $\hat P$ freely: analytic curve
deformations see the $\zeta(3)$ level and are structurally blind to the
$\zeta(5)$ level --- matching the family's weight spike $\{0,3,5\}$.
\end{theorem}

The same fact appears on the formal side: the one known
$P$-emitting deformation (``family 1'') has letter data that is not
curve-consistent, and admits no $\Gamma$-product realisation at any
reasonable shift scale (minimal: $\varepsilon/120$ with multiplicities
$\sim10^{11}$) \VerifiedR{\texttt{eps40}}. Blindness and
non-realizability are one fact seen from two sides: \emph{the top graded
piece requires jet data decoupled from lower jets}.

\subsection{The tool}
Decoupled jets exist analytically: mixed parameter-derivatives of the
cellular integral. What they need is an evaluation anchor, and its first
rung was tested and passed:
\[
\Lbz(\dot Q)(n)=-\sum_{i=0}^3c_i'(n)\,Q(n+i),
\qquad
\dot Q=\textstyle\sum_{k,l}T\cdot\Lambda_1^{(n)},
\]
exactly \VerifiedR{exact $\Q$, $n\le18$; \texttt{eps46}}, with a purely
discrete proof route (differentiate the rational certificate; the $k$-
and $l$-variations vanish identically --- they are gauge). The tower has
an honest rung-two obstruction at the sum level (continuation cells
revive under second derivatives; seven of nine letter directions are
safe, $n{-}k,n{-}l$ are not, and the $B_5$ weight as tabulated needs
order-4 unsafe jets), with two identified escapes: a null representative
of $B_5$ with safe orders, or the integral-level tower licensed by
Brown--Zudilin's own transfer principle, for which the explicit
continuous-parameter contiguity matrices in the appendix of Brown's
Mellin-transform paper are the template (\VA; memos
\texttt{VARIATIONAL\_TOWER.md}, \texttt{BROWN26\_MINING.md}).

\begin{lesson}
The proof of an obstruction can name the missing instrument. Here it
named two: polarized (decoupled-jet) objects for the $\zeta(5)$ row, and
--- via the question ``what \emph{does} the jet mechanism see?'' --- the
discovery instrument of the next act.
\end{lesson}

\section{Act III: the discovery instrument, its negatives, and a
refutation}\label{sec:instrument}

Run in reverse (scan $\to$ rational reconstruction $\to$ exact held-out
validation, always with a positive control), the jet calculus is a
companion-discovery instrument.

\emph{Controls.} On Franel and $\mathbf D$ --- known companions --- the
pinned scan rediscovers reachability from linear atoms, minimal support,
and the reconstructed weights verify exactly over $\Q$ on held-out ranges;
they differ from the published formulas by proved-null weights, i.e.\ new
equivalent representations \VerifiedR{exact $\Q$, $n\le25$;
\texttt{eps43}}. (The control also caught a genuine int64 overflow ---
the standing argument for controls.)

\emph{Negatives with structure.} The three families for which the
sporadic status paper had proved no fit exists ($\mathbf B,\delta,\zeta$)
are jet-unreachable \ExcludedR{two primes, linear+quadratic curves,
$\varepsilon^2$ and $\varepsilon^3$, every $\zeta$-grade}. The paper's
three never-run full-degree fits were then run --- families $\zeta$,
$\eta$ at full degree, $\mathbf F$ at its seven tame arguments, pure and
character-twisted --- and all six are inconsistent, with excess
$90$--$173$ \ExcludedR{\texttt{eps44}; precise ansatz definition and the
modular-to-rational transfer lemma in \MA}. The complete tame
degree-$\le3$ ansatz is thereby exhausted for all seven conjectural
families. Two diagnostic corrections emerged: $\zeta$ \emph{does} have
summand--letter $\Q$-relations at full degree (a 15-dimensional kernel;
the old ``rank $=$ columns'' claim fails for $\zeta$), while $\eta$ is
maximally rigid even at $2530$ columns.

\emph{The kernel pays.} Extracting $\zeta$'s kernel produced two per-cell
annihilation laws; the first is now a theorem:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ann}
\sum_lS_\zeta(n,k,l)\bigl(\HH1k-2\HH1l+\HH1{n-l}\bigr)=0
\quad(0\le k\le n)
\qquad\Proved
\end{equation}
by an order-2 Zeilberger certificate with symbolically verified
certificate identities, endpoint vanishing, and unconditional induction
(\texttt{eps45}; stated as a Proposition in \MA) --- the first proved
structural fact about any conjectural-seven family's letters. The second
law remains \VerifiedR{$n\le25$} with a documented obstruction record
\Open. Moral: every fit for $\zeta$ had been conducted in a provably
degenerate alphabet.

\emph{A refutation, reported with pride.} The first form of the
reachability dichotomy --- jet-reachable $\iff$ principal character with
real limit --- made a prediction; its designed discriminating experiment
(the Domb family $\alpha$, and $\varepsilon$: principal character, real
limits, \emph{and} known harmonic companions) returned unreachable at
both primes \Refuted\ (\texttt{eps54}). The surviving invariant is the
\emph{level of the uniformization}: squarefree ($5,6$) reachable;
non-squarefree ($8,9,12,12,36$) not --- seven data points, no exceptions
--- and jet-reachability is strictly finer than formula existence: the
jets detect the arithmetic of the uniformization, not the letters of the
formula. The refuted conjecture is kept on the record in both papers.

\begin{lesson}
The campaign's conjectures are required to make predictions, and its
experiments are designed to kill them. One died. Its successor is better.
This is the methodology working, not failing.
\end{lesson}

\section{Act IV: modular anchors --- the centerpiece}\label{sec:modular}

The refuted conjecture's successor pointed at uniformizations, so the
campaign built the instrument that computes them: from a recurrence, the
Frobenius basis $y_0$, $y_1=y_0\log t+g$, the nome
$q=t\exp(g/y_0)$, the mirror map $t(q)$, and $F(q)=y_0(t(q))$ --- with
the Ap\'ery pair as validation (the instrument reproduces Beukers'
classical $\Gamma_0(6)$ eta parametrization exactly; \EA\ walks a reader
through this case with tables and code).

\subsection{Identifications}
All fifteen nome expansions are integral at scale one, and twelve of
fifteen $(t,F)$ pairs match explicit (generalized-)eta objects to
$q^{25}$ \VerifiedR{\texttt{eps51}; dictionary memo}. Statuses,
per the standing rules:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item $\zeta$: \Proved. $F_\zeta=\eta_3^{10}/(\eta_1\eta_9)^3
=\tfrac18(9E_2(q^9)-E_2(q))$ is an identity in $M_2(\Gamma_0(9))$
(Ligozat conditions; Sturm bound $2$; agreement to $q^{40}$) (\MA).
\item $\mathbf B$: $F_{\mathbf B}=b(-q)$, the Borwein cubic theta
$\eta(q)^3/\eta(q^3)$ at $-q$ \VerifiedR{all 27 computed coefficients},
with a level-36 half-integral shift anomaly and an equivalent untwisted
level-9 representative found by the census.
\item $\delta$: level 12 eta quotients for both $t$ and $F$ ---
apparently the first modular data ever attached to $\delta$ --- with the
identification proved \emph{modulo one hypothesis} (the degree-one
property of $t_\delta$ on the correct Atkin--Lehner extension); the
eta-side annihilator is exactly one-dimensional and equals $\delta$'s
operator coefficientwise (\texttt{DELTA\_MODULAR\_THEOREM.md}).
\item $\eta$: $[\eta(q)^5/\eta(q^5)](-q)$, level 20
\VerifiedR{all 27 coefficients}. Cooper's three: integral, not eta-type
in this coordinate \Open.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The theory: inversion, rectification, and the all-$n$
companion formula}
The centerpiece is now proved end to end (\MA):

\begin{theorem}[companion inversion]\label{thm:inv}
\Thm\quad If an order-$r$ operator is exactly rectified by its nome,
$L=C(t)\,F\,\thq^rF^{-1}$, then the normalized companion (the unique
log-free solution of $L(y_B)=t$ vanishing at $0$ --- the \emph{boundary
defect} equation, itself a two-line lemma) is
\[
y_B=F\,\thq^{-r}\Bigl(\frac t{C\,F}\Bigr).
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[rectification for the fifteen; the six symmetric
squares]\label{thm:sym2}
\Proved\quad Order-2 operators are rectified by Frobenius theory (nine
families, Cooper's $d\neq0$ rows included). Each of the six third-order
sporadic operators equals $t^3P_3(t)$ times the symmetric square of
\[
\widetilde D=\theta^2-t\Bigl(2a\theta^2+a\theta+\tfrac b2\Bigr)
+c\,t^2\Bigl(\theta+\tfrac12\Bigr)^2,
\]
uniformly in Zagier's parameters $(a,b,c)$ --- exact residual-zero
identities in $\Q(t)$, family by family (\texttt{eps57}). (Rectification
at $r\ge3$ is strictly stronger than maximal unipotent monodromy; the
referee's insistence on this distinction, and the resulting repair, are
part of the record.)
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[uniform companion formula, all $n$]\label{thm:comp}
\Thm\quad For every one of the fifteen sporadic pairs,
\[
B(n)=[t^n]\;F\cdot\thq^{-r}\Bigl(\frac{t\,\sigma^r}{P_r\,F}\Bigr)
\qquad\text{for all }n\ge0 .
\]
\end{theorem}

For the conjectural seven, whose tame harmonic ansatz is exhausted
(\S\ref{sec:instrument}), this is a companion formula in
provably-necessary new letters --- the first of any kind. Displayed for
the family that started it:
\[
\boxed{\;B_\zeta(n)=[t^n]\,F_\zeta\cdot\thq^{-3}
\Bigl(\frac{t\,\sigma^3}{P_3\,F_\zeta}\Bigr),\qquad
F_\zeta=\tfrac18\bigl(9E_2(q^9)-E_2(q)\bigr).\;}
\]
The companion problem was never about cleverer harmonic weights; it was
about whether the family's uniformization can be rationally eliminated.
Where it can, the Frobenius layer collapses to harmonic sums (the
classical formulas, which the construction reproduces); where it cannot,
the companion is an Eichler integral of the parametrizing form.

\subsection{The census}
Run in reverse over a precisely defined universe ($\sim5\times10^4$
hauptmodul/form pairs, fifteen genus-zero levels, stated banks, caps and
equivalence relation), the construction manufactures Ap\'ery-like pairs
--- and finds none new: the pipeline regenerates all ten identified
families from their $(t,F)$ data alone ($10/10$ exact), and every
integral three-term hit is a known sporadic or a classical degenerate
(Zagier's criterion $a^2-4c=0$ on the nose). \emph{No sixteenth pair in
this universe} \VerifiedR{census; \MA}. Four uncovered directions
(generalized-eta/$\Gamma_1$, Fricke hauptmoduls, order-4, levels $>25$)
are stated and reusable.

\section{Arithmetic shadows}\label{sec:arith}

\emph{Weight dichotomy.} All nine modular-weight-2 families satisfy the
classical $A(p)\equiv b_p\pmod p$ at every tested prime $p\le23$
\VerifiedR{\texttt{eps51}}, including the unidentified Cooper three.

\emph{A new weight-one law.} The six weight-one families fail the
classical congruence with an exactly structured defect:
\[
A(p)\;\equiv\;b_p-b\cdot\chi_N(p)\pmod p ,
\]
$b$ the recurrence's constant parameter and $\chi_N$ the quadratic
character of the \emph{level} (not the family: Franel has trivial family
character and a $\chi_{-3}$ level defect); $\mathbf D$ follows its own
mod-5 rule. Fitted on $p\le23$, the law \emph{passes all sixteen held-out
primes $29\le p\le97$ for every family}
\VerifiedR{\texttt{eps57\_heldout}} --- the referee's bar for ``credible
arithmetic evidence,'' met. The mechanism (Atkin--Swinnerton-Dyer
structure of the identified forms; for $\zeta$ the twist exponent equals
the nebentypus value at the discriminating primes) is stated as a
conjecture with $\zeta$ --- where the form is a theorem --- as the first
target (\MA).

\emph{The half-Ap\'ery numbers} (\HA). The symmetric-square theorem gives
Ap\'ery's numbers a square root, $u=\sqrt{y_0}$, and
$\ut_n:=4^n[t^n]u$ is an integer sequence:
$1,10,534,40900,3672550,\dots$ Its integrality, though, is
\emph{universal} --- $\sqrt{f(4t)}\in\Z[[t]]$ for any integral $f$ (a
two-line Catalan-number lemma) --- and the programme's earlier reading of
root-integrality as an arithmetic signal (including for the $\zeta(5)$
row) was corrected accordingly, in print. The genuine treasures sit one
layer deeper: $v_2(\ut_n)=s_2(n)$, the binary digit sum
\VerifiedR{$n\le200$}; and the \emph{split-prime Lucas law}
\[
\ut_{ap+r}\equiv\ut_a\ut_r\ (\mathrm{mod}\ p)
\iff
\bigl(\tfrac{-6}p\bigr)=1\ \text{or}\ p\in\{2,3\}
\]
--- a conjecture verified at fourteen primes with perfect correlation,
predicted from four and confirmed at ten --- with discriminant $-24$
exactly the quadratic datum of the weight-one eta identity
$u(t(q))=(\eta_2\eta_3)^{7/2}/(\eta_1\eta_6)^{5/2}$. The $\zeta(5)$ root
sequences share the 2-adic law and have \emph{no} Lucas law: the
split-prime congruence is a fingerprint of the weight-one world, not of
root-taking. A small object, found by accident, deflated honestly, and
still carrying a class field.

\section{The $\zeta(5)$ frontier}\label{sec:frontier}

What the campaign established about the Brown--Zudilin family itself, and
what remains:

\emph{Local signatures} \Proved: the order-9 generating operator has
$t=0$ exponents $0^{(5)},\tfrac12$, and an irrational cubic --- a
five-dimensional unipotent block, the local signature matching weight 5.

\emph{Arithmetic signatures} \VerifiedR{$q^{26}$/$q^{32}$}: the block's
mirror map and Yukawa-type datum are integral at scale one
($t(q)=q-94q^2-\cdots$; $K(q)=87q+33895q^2+\cdots$, with $K_1=87=4P_1$).
Root-integrality is \emph{withdrawn} as evidence (universal; \HA). The
$q$-normal form $\thq^5+K_3\thq^3+\cdots$ is displayed in \MA;
$K\not\equiv0$ is precisely the obstruction to bare inversion at $r=5$,
and $K$'s gauge-invariance is honestly \Open.

\emph{Sharp negatives} \Excluded: no order-5 right factor of $t$-degree
$\le20$ splits the block globally; the naive Dwork congruence fails (three
Frobenius slopes; the slope-refined form is the open target); the naive
elliptic-style row avatars have growing denominators --- calibrated
against the same pipeline recovering Beukers' weight-4 Eisenstein
combination exactly for Ap\'ery.

\emph{The two roads to the bridge.} (i) The mirror road:
Conjecture (\MA) --- in the block's normal-form coordinates the rows
$\hat P_n,P_n$ are Eichler-type objects in $F,K,\hat g_3,\dots$, and
\eqref{eq:bridge} is the $t$-shadow of a $q$-side identity; the first
computation (the \emph{proved} middle row against $\{F,K,\hat g_3\}$,
with the $K$-corrected variation-of-parameters inversion) is in progress
at the time of writing, as is a systematic denominator harvest from the
companion formula (\texttt{eps58}, \texttt{eps59}). (ii) The variational
road: the tower of \S\ref{sec:blind}, whose remaining one-time cost is
the parametric certificate, with Brown's contiguity matrices as template.

\emph{Calibration, stated plainly.} None of this touches the worthiness
exponent. The linear forms decay like $e^{-2.47n}$; the denominators grow
like $e^{5n}$; Brown--Zudilin's $0.86$ is best known and their analysis
caps known constructions short of irrationality. The campaign improved
the \emph{arithmetic} side --- companions, congruences, and the
denominator machinery those feed --- which is the only known lever on the
exponent, and a lever with a hard ceiling. The path to the irrationality
of $\zeta(5)$ remains unopened; anyone reporting otherwise is reporting
scenery.
