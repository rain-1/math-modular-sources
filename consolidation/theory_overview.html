0. The object and the one inequality

An Apéry system for a real number ξ is a pair of sequences An ∈ ℤ, Bn ∈ ℚ, both solutions of one linear recurrence with polynomial coefficients, such that

A_n ≍ λ₁ⁿ, |A_n ξ − B_n| ≍ λ₂ⁿ, d_nᵏ B_n ∈ ℤ (d_n = lcm(1..n) ≍ eⁿ).

Everything downstream is governed by one inequality:

ξ ∉ ℚ if λ₂ · eᵏ < 1, i.e. score := log(1/λ₂) − k > 0.

The three quantities are, in the modular picture, controlled by three separate pieces of data — and that separation is the first thing the project got genuinely right. The special value ξ is decided by an Eisenstein (or cusp) form, the exponent λ₂ by the position of singular points of a modular function, and the denominator depth k by how many Eichler integrations are needed minus how many come for free. The "three layers" of the GPT-era book are a dressed-up version of exactly this split; I'll keep the split and drop the dressing.

The Catalan row is the important one to stare at. Every scalar modular Catalan system found so far has λ₂ > 1: the ratio Bn/An converges but the linear form grows. That single fact is why the Catalan work had to move to two rows (section 5), and it is a geometric fact about the level-8 curve, not bad luck.

1. The modular dictionary

The dictionary is classical and I regard it as the bedrock. lit Beukers (1987), Zagier (2009), Beauville (1982), Almkvist–Zudilin, Cooper.

Γ ⊂ SL₂(ℝ) genus-zero (commensurable with SL₂(ℤ) in every case we use) t(τ) Hauptmodul with integral q-expansion ("mirror map") f(τ) modular form of weight w, integral q-expansion, with Σ A_n t(τ)ⁿ = f(τ) (A_n ∈ ℤ because t and f are integral)

The Picard–Fuchs operator L of f in the variable t has order w+1 and solutions f·τj, 0 ≤ j ≤ w. Its singular points are the images under t of the cusps and elliptic points of Γ. Weight 1 gives the second-order (ζ(2)-type) systems; weight 2 gives third order; and the six sporadic third-order operators are exactly the symmetric squares of six second-order ones proved (ledger C02), which is the differential-equation shadow of Sym²H of the elliptic Gauss–Manin system. Higher odd zeta values live on Sym^{w}H: ζ(5) needs weight 4 (f = g⁴ for a weight-1 g), ζ(7) weight 6.

Two classification facts anchor the low-order census. lit Beauville: there are exactly six semistable rational elliptic surfaces with four singular fibres, and they are Zagier's six sporadic second-order families. verified ledger C09: a census of ~5·10⁴ eta/Eisenstein candidates at small level finds no sixteenth sporadic pair in that universe. The honest status of "classification of integral modular Apéry pairs" is therefore: complete for the geometric reason in weight 1, census-level above.

2. The source theorem: where the special value comes from

This is the project's central proved contribution and the one I would lead with in any publication.

proved Companion law (ledger C01–C04, E01–E02; Eisenstein Source Theorem with a Lean project). For each of the fifteen sporadic pairs the second solution is

B(t) = f · D^{−(w+1)} Φ, Φ := f · D t, D = q d/dq,

and for the twelve modular families the source Φ is a holomorphic Eisenstein series of weight w+2 with zero cuspidal projection. For Cooper's s₇, s₁₀, s₁₈ the source is meromorphic (a principal-part correction is needed).

Why this produces L-values: the Eichler integral D^{−(w+1)}Φ of a weight-(w+2) form has a period polynomial whose coefficients are the critical values L(Φ, j), 1 ≤ j ≤ w+1. For an Eisenstein series L(Φ,s) factors as a product of two Dirichlet L-functions times a finite Dirichlet polynomial, so the critical values are Dirichlet L-values and powers of π. The Apéry limit is the value of the Eichler integral at the fold — the point of the modular curve whose image is the first singularity t₁ of L — and the fold connection lemma proved (E03) expresses it through the period at that point. In the Fricke-symmetric cases the fold is the Fricke fixed point, a CM point, and the limit is the "top" critical value L(Φ, w+1).

This gives a clean dichotomy that I think deserves to be stated as the organising principle:

conj Target principle. For a modular Apéry system with source Φ, the Apéry limit is a rational (or algebraic) combination of the critical values L(Φ, j). Eisenstein sources give Dirichlet L-values L(r, χ); cuspidal sources give critical values of cusp forms. The Domb apparatus (S3) is the first cuspidal instance: B*n/An → L(f₆, 3)/2 with f₆ = (η₁η₂η₃η₆)² verified (61 digits), with a phase-0 document claiming the exact factor.

Proved for the fifteen; the principle itself is what the classification programme should be trying to prove in general.

Period annihilation is finite Hecke linear algebra

Apéry's E₄ − 28E₄(2τ) + 63E₄(3τ) − 36E₄(6τ) is not a coincidence. An oldform combination C = Σ c_d V_d multiplies the L-function by P_C(s) = Σ c_d d^{−s}, so killing the unwanted critical values (the powers of π) is solving a Vandermonde system in d^{−2}. proved (book §5; verified in verify/period_annihilation.gp): the null space is barycentric, the one-prime projectors are Π(1 − p^{2a}V_p) with Gaussian-binomial coefficients, and Fricke parity halves the conditions. Outputs that are new and checked: the level-16 ζ(5) projector, the level-12 ζ(7) "parent" with target 209/1728·ζ(7), the level-24 β(4) doublet, and the level-24 L(4,χ₋₃) pair from Shvets's symmetric cube. Franke (2024) has the general theory of Eisenstein spaces with prescribed critical vanishing; our projectors are its explicit Hecke-generated slice. This is elementary, it is right, and it is generative — it is the part of the book that produced new systems on demand.

3. Convergence is geometry; denominators are integrations

Now the other two knobs, which are where irrationality is won or lost.

λ₂ and the singular points. A_n ≍ t₁^{−n} where t₁ is the singularity of L nearest to 0, and B − ξA is precisely the solution that continues analytically past t₁; its growth is t₂^{−n} for the next singularity. So λ₂ = 1/|t₂|. In Apéry's cases a Fricke involution acts on t as t ↦ 1/(c·t), forcing t₁t₂ = 1/c and hence a palindromic recurrence with λ₂ = 1/λ₁ < 1. When the fold is not Fricke-symmetric (the Catalan E family, singularities at 1/8 and 1/4) there is no such pairing and λ₂ > 1. This is the geometric statement underlying every "it converges but proves nothing" result in the archive: a single scalar row can only beat the denominators when the cusp/elliptic-point configuration of (Γ, t) puts the second singular point outside the disc of radius e^{k}.

k and free integrations. Each D^{−1} costs at most one factor d_n proved (denominator-transfer lemma, phase 0), so generically k = w+1: 2 for ζ(2), 3 for ζ(3), 5 for ζ(5). Cooper's families show the exception: (n+1) | A_n proved (Bogner) makes the first integration free and drops k by one. The mechanism is that the source, or its first primitive, is already integral in the t-lattice. Engineering free integrations is the right lever on the denominator side; the project tried it (ζ(7) at level 60: filtered depth 19/6 against log R = 3.464, margin +0.30 verified, but the lift that would realise the depth drop is obstructed open). That near-miss is the single most interesting scalar lead in the archive and deserves an independent audit.

conj Scalar barrier. For trivial character and Sym^{w}-type systems on genus-zero Γ ⊂ SL₂(ℝ) commensurable with the modular group, log|t₂| − (w+1) < 0 for all w ≥ 3; the Apéry level-6 case is the unique positive score at w = 2 among integral eta-quotient Hauptmoduls of level ≤ 25 verified (E14). The reason is that raising the level crowds the cusps into the unit q-disc and shrinks |t₂| while the weight needed for ζ(2m+1) grows linearly.

Proving a version of this would be a real theorem and would formally justify abandoning scalar searches for ζ(5) — which is what the dead ends in the archive amount to, empirically.

4. Isogenies, Hecke, CM: the geometric layer that is real

  • lit Oldform operators V_d are degeneracy maps, i.e. cyclic-isogeny correspondences on the universal elliptic curve; they act on H and so on every Sym^{w}H. Period annihilation therefore has a geometric meaning: the annihilated source is a linear combination of isogeny pullbacks of one Eisenstein class. proved the functoriality L(CΦ, s) = P_C(s)L(Φ, s) and its transport to the Eichler primitive, C ↦ Σ c_d d^{−(w+1)}V_d.
  • proved Prime completions (1 − p²V_p)Φ of the level-6 ζ(3) source give new genus-zero systems at p = 5, 7, 11; the sign-refined Fricke count predicted the hidden ζ(7) parent at level 12 whose 2-completion is the known level-24 system. Classification → prediction genuinely happened here.
  • proved The fold is a CM point; evaluating the Eichler integral there gives Ramanujan-type Lambert identities. The β(4) identity at τ = i/√24 holds to 40 digits (verify/beta4_lambert.py). One could mass-produce such identities; their interest is that they are the "CM shadow" of the extension class.
  • conj Orientation and vector-valuedness. For nontrivial χ there are two Eisenstein orientations E_{k,χ,1}, E_{k,1,χ} exchanged by Fricke. When the Fricke/translation action on the orientation span has no invariant line (Catalan at level 20: dihedral of order 8) no scalar system exists and one is forced to vector-valued (Hermite–Padé) systems. This is the one place where "Layer B" language earns its keep: it is the statement that the extension class, not the local system, decides scalar vs vector.

What is not real yet: the "adelic Layer C", free-depth flags, τ_F, syntomic κ_p, and the "global three-layer functoriality theorem" of V10/V11. Those are definitions plus one calibration (reading CDT's numbers 14, 3, 1 back into a formula). I would keep them as a programme appendix, not as theory.

5. Beyond one row: the routes that can actually cross the line

5a. Two realisations, one period, aligned lattices (the Catalan mechanism)

This is the most original idea in the whole corpus and the one closest to a new theorem. Take two Apéry systems for the same ξ from different sources — Zudilin's hypergeometric Catalan row and the level-8 modular row. Neither beats its denominators alone. But both B/A ratios converge 2-adically to the same 2-adic period, at explicit different rates, so the 2×2 determinant

Δ_{m,n} = A_m B'_n − A'_n B_m has v₂(Δ_{5n,8n}) ≥ 20n − 1 − 2|s₂(5n) − s₂(n)|

verified exactly at n = 100 (1993 = predicted). A congruence lattice built from this, plus Minkowski's second theorem on two successive minima proved (D02, the genuinely new geometry-of-numbers step), turns two useless rows into a construction of quality 0.6588 (LCM version, Lean-checked) and 0.9025 (2-adic version, one lemma conditional), and River reports a 1−ε version now under Lean audit.

conj p-adic coincidence principle. Two modular Apéry systems realising the same Eisenstein extension class (same L(r,χ) target, sources differing by an oldform operator C with P_C(r) ≠ 0, or by a hypergeometric/modular change of realisation) have companion ratios converging p-adically to a common p-adic L-value for the primes where both lattices are Frobenius-compatible, with rates computable from their twisted Lucas laws (ledger H03, H09, P01–P03). The resulting determinant valuations are the input to a multi-lattice construction.

This is the theorem I would try to build: it links the source theory (which realisations exist), the p-adic strand (rates, Λ-algebra), and the Diophantine strand (successive minima). It is also exactly what "two representations whose arithmetic lattices line up" means in precise terms. The p=3 attempts in the archive that failed are informative: they show the alignment is a property of the pair of realisations, not something one gets by changing the prime.

5b. Arithmetic holonomy (Calegari–Dimitrov–Tang)

lit Replace one linear form by a vector of functions with a common analytic continuation to a larger domain; a holonomy-rank bound contradicts functional independence. CDT's proof for 1, ζ(2), L(2,χ₋₃) uses exactly the two Eisenstein orientations (1−8V₂)E_{3,χ₋₃,1} and (1−V₂)E_{3,1,χ₋₃} proved (book §8a) — i.e. the inner and outer sources of the classification, deliberately not fully purified. The lesson is structural: full period annihilation is optimal for a scalar row and suboptimal for holonomy, because the Tate direction supplies extra independent functions. The Catalan level-144 system was pushed to the CDT entry test and failed it (continuation radius 1.0708 against the needed 1.1506; ≥58 rows required) verified; that number is a reusable obstruction.

5c. Non-symmetric-power motives

Brown–Zudilin's ζ(5) approximants are not Sym⁴ of an elliptic family; the project's Frobenius/cellular-jet work (F01 Stokes-ratio formula proved, purity defect −3/π² proved, κ-vector closed forms verified to 434 digits) is an honest attempt to understand them. Its bottleneck (B01, the compact top-row formula) is a harmonic-sum identity, not modular. I'd keep this strand separate: it's where ζ(5) will come from if it comes, and the modular theory's contribution is negative (the scalar barrier) plus the Hecke/lattice ideas.

6. What the dead ends are worth

River's instinct is right that most GPT output is "I can't prove it, so let me prove I can't." But as a body it is a no-go census, and censuses are exactly what a classification theory needs. The ones I would keep, as reusable pruning with stated universes:

  • No scalar weight-6 source with vanishing constant term at every cusp on X₀⁺(169) (ζ(5) via X_s(13)); exhaustive finite-field certificates verified.
  • Catalan scalar systems at levels 16 and 32 are rigid and obstructed by a z log²z term; level 64 unexamined.
  • Γ₁(7) elliptic family: quotient defined over a real cubic field, Plücker minors nonzero ⇒ scalar wedge descent obstructed.
  • The ζ(5) "fusion" of 2- and 3-adic Lucas directions is killed by a defect theorem; all p=3 "slopes" for Catalan were unimodular-coordinate artefacts.
  • All 22 Ramanujan-Machine Catalan continued fractions collapse to one rank-2 level-8 module verified — a classification result disguised as a dead end.

7. The true theory, as I currently see it

Stripping the formalism, the theory has four statements. Two are proved for the known census, two are conjectural and are the research programme.

  1. Realisation. Integral modular Apéry pairs of symmetric-power type are classified by (Γ, t, f) with integral Hauptmodul and integral weight-w form; complete in weight 1 (Beauville), census-level in weight 2, open above. lit/verified
  2. Source. The companion is f·D^{−(w+1)}(f·Dt); the Apéry limit is a critical value of L(f·Dt, s); Eisenstein sources give Dirichlet L-values, cuspidal sources give cusp-form L-values; period annihilation is Hecke/oldform Vandermonde algebra. proved for the fifteen; conj in general.
  3. Score. score = log|t₂| − (w+1) + (free integrations), with t₂ determined by the cusp/elliptic-point geometry of (Γ,t) and Fricke symmetry, and free integrations by exactness of the source in the integral lattice. Scalar barrier for w ≥ 3. conj
  4. Multiplicity. Irrationality beyond the scalar barrier comes from several realisations of one extension class: p-adically aligned rows (multi-lattice), or a holonomy vector (CDT), and the alignment/independence is decided by the extension class and its Frobenius structure, not by the local system. conj

That is a theory a human can hold in their head, an AI can search with, and each line has a concrete proof target attached.

This is the direction with a real chance at a new irrationality result and it is where the Hecke/isogeny language is genuinely useful.
