# Research Program: A Mixed-Motive–Apéry–Holonomy Attack on the Irrationality of Catalan’s Constant

**Status:** Research programme, 23 August 2026  
**Principal target:** Prove
\[
G=\beta(2)=\sum_{n\ge0}\frac{(-1)^n}{(2n+1)^2}\notin\mathbf Q.
\]

## 1. Executive summary

The existing Catalan programme has identified a persistent obstruction. Classical hypergeometric, modular/Eichler, \(\mu_4\)-polylogarithmic, Atkin–Lehner, and direct cellular realizations can all produce Catalan's constant, often with excellent analytic or arithmetic properties, but the same effective weight-two denominator cost repeatedly reappears before an irrationality criterion is reached.

A qualitatively different realization is now available.

Eskandari, Murty and Nemoto construct a **two-dimensional mixed motive over \(\mathbf Q\) having Catalan's constant as a period**, and use it to produce rational linear forms in \(1\) and \(G\).

This is exactly the type of realization anticipated by the earlier Catalan obstruction analysis: instead of trying to improve the same Eisenstein extension, isolate \(G\) inside a smaller motive whose period space is intrinsically

\[
\langle1,G\rangle_{\mathbf Q}.
\]

The proposed programme is to combine this new geometric motive with the Apéry/holonomy machinery developed in the present project:

\[
\boxed{
\text{two-dimensional Catalan motive}
\longrightarrow
\text{moving Gauss--Manin system}
\longrightarrow
\text{Picard--Fuchs equation}
\longrightarrow
\text{integral Apéry realization}
\longrightarrow
\text{adelic arithmetic holonomy}.
}
\]

The crucial possibility is that the **same Catalan extension class may have a substantially cheaper integral realization** than the previously studied level-8 and \(\mu_4\) hosts.

The project should therefore focus not on discovering another formula for \(G\), but on constructing and optimizing the arithmetic local system attached to this two-dimensional motive.

# 2. Background and present state of the problem

Catalan's constant

\[
G=L(2,\chi_{-4})
\]

is not known to be irrational.

Existing rational approximations remain far below what is needed for an irrationality proof. For example, Viola and Marcovecchio obtain effective approximants with exponent approximately \(0.6293\); this remains insufficient to establish irrationality.

Within the present Apéry-sequence programme, numerous Catalan realizations have been investigated:

- level-8 Eisenstein/Eichler-integral realizations;
- modular and Atkin–Lehner variants;
- \(\mu_4\)-polylogarithmic realizations;
- \(2\)-adic and multi-prime constructions;
- directional and positivity methods;
- three-period and two-class variants;
- hypergeometric and cellular integrals;
- symmetric-square elliptic realizations;
- signature-4 Gauss parameter deformations.

These experiments have given a fairly consistent negative diagnosis:

\[
\boxed{
\text{the principal obstruction is not finding }G;
\text{ it is the arithmetic cost of its realization.}
}
\]

The new programme is designed specifically to alter that realization cost.

# 3. The new geometric input

The recent work of Eskandari–Murty–Nemoto constructs a two-dimensional mixed motive over \(\mathbf Q\) whose periods include \(G\), and produces a family of linear forms

\[
a_F+b_FG,\qquad a_F,b_F\in\mathbf Q.
\]

This externally confirms the existence of a genuinely small motivic host for Catalan's constant rather than the much larger period modules arising naturally from \(\operatorname{Li}_2(i)\) or modular Eichler integrals.

A basic geometric period is

\[
\boxed{
G=
\iint_{\Delta}
\frac{dx\,dy}{1-x^2-y^2},
}
\]

where

\[
\Delta=\{(x,y):x\ge0,\ y\ge0,\ x+y\le1\}.
\]

The geometry carries the order-four symmetry

\[
\sigma(x,y)=(-y,x).
\]

After the appropriate geometric modifications and symmetry projection, the relevant period space collapses to the two-dimensional space generated by

\[
1,\qquad G.
\]

This is the feature that makes the construction particularly promising.

The goal is to **move this motive in a one-parameter family while retaining the two-dimensional quotient**.

# 4. Central research hypothesis

Let \(C\) denote the two-dimensional Catalan motive.

The main hypothesis of the programme is:

> **Catalan Realization Hypothesis.**  
> There exists a one-parameter geometric deformation \(C_z\) of \(C\) whose two-dimensional arithmetic local system admits an integral or globally bounded Frobenius solution and whose arithmetic-holonomy capacity is strictly large enough to contradict rationality of the connection coefficient \(G\).

Equivalently, we seek a realization in which

\[
\text{analytic continuation gain}
>
\text{integral-lattice cost}.
\]

This differs fundamentally from trying to improve a fixed sequence of rational approximants.

# 5. Natural moving period

The simplest deformation is

\[
\boxed{
\Phi(z)
=
\iint_\Delta
\frac{dx\,dy}
{1-z(x^2+y^2)}.
}
\]

It satisfies

\[
\Phi(1)=G.
\]

Put

\[
H(z)=z\Phi(z).
\]

A direct calculation gives

\[
\boxed{
H'(z)
=
\frac{
\operatorname{artanh}
\sqrt{\frac{z}{2-z}}
}{
\sqrt{z(2-z)}
}.
}
\]

Now introduce

\[
q=\sqrt{\frac{z}{2-z}},
\qquad
z=\frac{2q^2}{1+q^2}.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
H(z(q))
=
2\int_0^q
\frac{\operatorname{artanh}u}{1+u^2}\,du.
}
\]

The Catalan point \(z=1\) corresponds to \(q=1\), giving

\[
G=
2\int_0^1
\frac{\operatorname{artanh}u}{1+u^2}\,du.
\]

Thus the geometric motive is related to the familiar cyclotomic/dilogarithmic realization, but the geometric symmetry projection should retain only its two-dimensional quotient.

This distinction is essential:

\[
\boxed{
\text{full }\mu_4\text{ local system}
\neq
\text{two-dimensional Catalan quotient}.
}
\]

Arithmetic holonomy must be applied to the quotient, not to the full polylogarithmic host.

# 6. Work Package A: Construct the moving two-dimensional quotient

## Objective

Construct \(C_z\) geometrically and show that the \(\sigma\)-projection survives as a rank-two variation.

## Tasks

1. Define the relative algebraic surface/pair underlying
   \[
   \frac{dx\,dy}{1-z(x^2+y^2)}.
   \]

2. Resolve the moving intersection points of
   \[
   1-z(x^2+y^2)=0
   \]
   with the boundary divisor.

3. Extend the order-four action
   \[
   \sigma:(x,y)\mapsto(-y,x)
   \]
   to the resolved family.

4. Compute the induced action on relative de Rham cohomology.

5. Construct the projector selecting the two-dimensional Catalan summand.

6. Verify that specialization at \(z=1\) recovers the known Catalan motive.

## Desired output

A rank-two vector bundle with connection

\[
(\mathcal C,\nabla)
\]

over an explicit punctured \(z\)-line.

## Critical question

Does the two-dimensional quotient exist globally as a connection, or only at the individual fiber \(z=1\)?

This is the first genuine go/no-go test.

# 7. Work Package B: Compute the Gauss–Manin connection

Choose a de Rham basis

\[
\omega_0(z),\qquad \omega_1(z)
\]

for the projected quotient.

Compute

\[
\nabla
\begin{pmatrix}
\omega_0\\
\omega_1
\end{pmatrix}
=
A(z)
\begin{pmatrix}
\omega_0\\
\omega_1
\end{pmatrix}dz
\]

with

\[
A(z)\in M_2(\mathbf Q(z)).
\]

The computation should be performed exactly, preferably by Griffiths–Dwork reduction in the resolved relative cohomology.

Required outputs:

- exact singular locus;
- residue matrices;
- local exponents;
- monodromy type;
- determinant local system;
- apparent singularities, if any;
- behavior at \(z=0,1,2,\infty\).

A particularly favorable outcome would be a rigid rank-two Fuchsian system.

The primary hope is that the projected motive has differential rank \(2\), rather than inheriting the larger rank of the full dilogarithmic representation.

# 9. Work Package D: Find the integral Apéry realization

Let the holomorphic solution at \(z=0\) be

\[
A(z)=\sum_{n\ge0}A_nz^n.
\]

Determine the smallest integer \(\lambda\) such that

\[
\lambda^nA_n\in\mathbf Z
\]

or, more generally, determine the exact denominator function.

The target is a globally bounded \(G\)-function with unusually small scaling constant.

For the extension solution

\[
B(z)=\sum_{n\ge0}B_nz^n,
\]

compute the exact lattice depth:

\[
D_nB_n\in\mathbf Z.
\]

Candidate behavior of greatest interest is

\[
D_n\mid C^n d_n
\]

rather than

\[
C^nd_n^2.
\]

A genuine drop from two LCM layers to one would constitute the main arithmetic breakthrough.

# 10. Work Package E: Identify the Catalan companion

Construct two canonical solutions \(A_n,B_n\) of the resulting recurrence so that

\[
\boxed{
\frac{B_n}{A_n}\longrightarrow G
}
\]

or an explicitly rational multiple of \(G\).

The equality must follow from the geometry/connection matrix, not merely from numerical recognition.

Required proof:

1. identify the extension period at \(z=1\);
2. prove that the period plane is exactly
   \[
   \mathbf Q+\mathbf QG;
   \]
3. normalize the companion;
4. establish the limiting ratio.

This would produce an **Apéry sequence intrinsic to the new two-dimensional Catalan motive**.

# 11. Benchmark: the newly discovered cyclotomic Apéry system

A useful comparison object has already emerged from the present investigation.

Define

\[
C_n=
\int_0^1\!\!\int_0^1
\frac{
x^{2n}(1-x^4)^n
y^{2n}(1-y^4)^n
}
{(1+x^2y^2)^{2n+1}}
\,dx\,dy.
\]

Structural congruence considerations give

\[
C_n=A_nG+B_n,
\qquad A_n,B_n\in\mathbf Q,
\]

with no \(\pi\) or \(\log2\) contamination.

Set

\[
U_n=(-1)^n16^nA_n,
\qquad
V_n=(-1)^n16^nB_n.
\]

Experimentally,

\[
U_0=1,\qquad U_1=28,\qquad
U_2=2596,\qquad
U_3=311536,\ldots
\]

and both \(U_n\) and \(V_n\) satisfy

\[
\begin{aligned}
&(n+2)^2(2n+3)^2(20n^2+32n+13)X_{n+2}\\
&\quad-4P(n)X_{n+1}\\
&\quad-256(n+1)^2(2n+1)^2
(20n^2+72n+65)X_n=0,
\end{aligned}
\]

where

\[
\begin{aligned}
P(n)=
&3520n^6+26752n^5+83024n^4\\
&+134592n^3+120196n^2+56088n+10699.
\end{aligned}
\]

The dominant characteristic roots are

\[
\boxed{\lambda_\pm=88\pm40\sqrt5.}
\]

The recessive magnitude is

\[
|\lambda_-|
=
40\sqrt5-88
=
1.442719\ldots>1.
\]

Thus this row does not prove irrationality.

Furthermore the companion appears to obey

\[
\operatorname{den}(V_n)\mid d_{2n}^2.
\]

Nevertheless, this sequence is important for three reasons:

- it gives a clean new Apéry architecture for \(G\);
- it provides a benchmark against which the EMN-derived sequence can be measured;
- it demonstrates that the desired period purification \(\mathbf Q+\mathbf QG\) is compatible with second-order holonomy.

A creative-telescoping proof of this recurrence should be completed independently.

# 13. Work Package G: Compute the \(p\)-adic realization

For every relevant good prime \(p\):

1. construct the crystalline/rigid realization of \(C_z\);
2. compute Frobenius on the two-dimensional quotient;
3. identify ordinary and exceptional primes;
4. compute overconvergence radii \(R_p\);
5. determine the integral Frobenius lattice;
6. test Dwork-type tower congruences;
7. compute slopes and possible extension collisions.

The primary invariant is not merely a congruence but the **net local gain**

\[
\sigma_p-\kappa_p,
\]

where

- \(\sigma_p\) measures overconvergence/Frobenius gain;
- \(\kappa_p\) measures the new denominator cost caused by the chosen realization.

Only primes with positive net contribution should enter the adelic inequality.

Special attention should be given to \(p=2\), but the construction should not be allowed to depend exclusively on an optimistic \(2\)-adic estimate.

# 14. Work Package H: Arithmetic-holonomy inequality

Having obtained the exact local system, construct the Bost–Charles/CDT arithmetic-holonomy datum.

Determine:

\[
m=\dim(\text{relevant section space}),
\]

the archimedean capacity term

\[
\mathcal E_\infty,
\]

the finite-prime gains

\[
\mathcal E_p,
\]

and the arithmetic lattice cost

\[
\tau.
\]

The target inequality has the schematic form

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal E_\infty+\sum_p\mathcal E_p+\tau<m.
}
\]

The enormous potential advantage of the new motive is that one may have

\[
m=2
\]

or only slightly larger, rather than the much larger function inventory required by the full \(\mu_4\) realization.

This must be computed from the projected motive itself.

One must **not** re-embed it into the full dilogarithmic module before applying holonomy, since that would destroy the principal advantage of the construction.

# 15. Rationality-dependent mechanism

Ultimately the proof must exploit the assumption

\[
G=\frac ab\in\mathbf Q.
\]

The preferred mechanism is:

\[
G\in\mathbf Q
\Longrightarrow
\text{extension class becomes rationally constrained}
\Longrightarrow
\text{extra algebraic section / fold / dependence}
\Longrightarrow
\text{arithmetic-holonomy contradiction}.
\]

This needs to be formulated precisely in one of three possible ways.

### Mechanism I: conditional splitting

Show that rational \(G\) forces the Betti–de Rham extension to split over a rational lattice.

### Mechanism II: conditional connection cancellation

Find a connection coefficient of the form

\[
qG-p
\]

whose vanishing removes a logarithmic or additive continuation obstruction.

### Mechanism III: conditional linear dependence

Construct enough globally defined sections that \(G\in\mathbf Q\) lowers their dimension, contradicting a strict arithmetic-holonomy dimension inequality.

Mechanism III currently appears the most robust.

Treat \(t\) as an optimization parameter.

\[
t\sim\theta N

The objective function should combine

\[
\text{saddle decay}

\text{\(2\)-adic scaling}

This should be treated as a continuous asymptotic optimization problem before searching for exact integer parameter sequences.

# 17. Relation with the Gauss-parameter realization

Another newly identified realization is

\[
F_a(t)={}_2F_1(a,1-a;1;t)
\]

and

\[
D(t)=
\left.
\partial_aF_a(t)
\right|_{a=1/4}.
\]

Catalan's constant occurs in the \(t\leftrightarrow1-t\) connection matrix through

\[
\psi_1(1/4)-\psi_1(3/4)=16G.
\]

The remarkable arithmetic feature is that after the natural scaling,

\[
d_n[x^n]D(64x)\in\mathbf Z.
\]

Thus the basic deformation has only one LCM layer.

However, extracting \(G\) cleanly from its connection matrix introduces nuisance periods such as \(\log2\), and the missing arithmetic depth reappears in the regularized companion.

This realization should therefore be retained as a **local model of the extension**, not currently as the principal irrationality host.

A useful subsidiary goal is to prove that the EMN two-dimensional quotient is an intrinsic motivic projection of this larger Gauss/polylogarithmic picture.

Such an identification would explain why the new motive removes the unwanted period directions.

# 18. Conceptual synthesis

The programme is based on the distinction

\[
\boxed{
\text{period}
\neq
\text{extension class}
\neq
\text{integral realization}.
}
\]

All of the Catalan constructions may represent the same underlying regulator class, while exhibiting radically different:

- ranks;
- denominator depths;
- Frobenius slopes;
- singularity patterns;
- conformal radii;
- holonomy dimensions.

Therefore the objective is not to find a “better identity for \(G\).”

It is to find the realization

\[
(C,[G],\Lambda)
\]

whose lattice \(\Lambda\) optimizes the global Diophantine inequality.

**Key statistic:**

Compute local data at the first several hundred good primes.

### Stage 8 — Holonomy

Only after the exact arithmetic data are known, evaluate the full adelic inequality.

# 20. Success criteria

There are three levels of success.

## Level I: New Apéry system

Produce an explicit recurrence

\[
P_2(n)X_{n+2}+P_1(n)X_{n+1}+P_0(n)X_n=0
\]

with primary and companion solutions satisfying

\[
A_n\in\mathbf Z,
\qquad
D_nB_n\in\mathbf Z,
\qquad
\frac{B_n}{A_n}\to G.
\]

This is already publishable if the system is genuinely attached to the new motive.

## Level II: New structural theorem

Prove that the EMN motive is the minimal two-dimensional quotient of one or more familiar Catalan realizations and establish the resulting archimedean/\(p\)-adic regulator dictionary.

This would integrate the new result into the general theory of Apéry extensions.

## Level III: Irrationality

Obtain either

\[
D_n|A_nG-B_n|\to0
\]

with nonzero integer linear forms, or a strict arithmetic-holonomy inequality under the hypothesis \(G\in\mathbf Q\).

Then

\[
\boxed{G\notin\mathbf Q.}
\]

# 21. Failure criteria

The following outcomes should cause a route to be terminated rather than endlessly optimized.

### F1. Hidden depth-two invariant

If every integral realization of the projected extension satisfies asymptotically

\[
D_n\gtrsim d_n^2
\]

and no compensating \(p\)-adic gain exists, record this as an invariant obstruction.

### F2. Rank inflation

If moving the motive necessarily restores the entire \(\mu_4\) period module, the rank-two advantage disappears.

### F3. No rationality-sensitive monodromy

If \(G\) occurs only as a terminal boundary value and rationality produces no change in global holonomy, CDT is unlikely to help.

### F4. Insufficient conformal radius

If the nearest singularity forces analytic decay below the exact denominator exponent even after all available covers, stop that realization.

### F5. Neutral \(p\)-adic slopes

Large-looking congruences are irrelevant if the new denominator cost cancels them. Always score the net quantity.

# 22. Principal conjectures

### Conjecture A — Moving Catalan quotient

The two-dimensional Catalan motive extends to a rank-two arithmetic variation \(C_z\) containing the period \(\Phi(z)\).

### Conjecture B — Apéry realization

The variation \(C_z\) has an integral cusp at which its primary solution is globally bounded and its companion has arithmetic depth strictly smaller than that of the classical Catalan constructions.

### Conjecture C — Motivic equivalence

The Gauss-parameter, EMN, cyclotomic-cellular and modular Catalan constructions realize the same regulator class but different integral lattices.

### Conjecture D — Holonomy improvement

The arithmetic-holonomy dimension/capacity of the rank-two EMN quotient is strictly better than that of the full \(\mu_4\) host.

### Conjecture E — Irrationality criterion

Under \(G\in\mathbf Q\), the projected local system acquires an additional rational dependence incompatible with the combined archimedean and \(p\)-adic holonomy bounds.

# 23. Why this programme is qualitatively new

Previous Catalan attacks largely had the form

\[
\text{choose a known expression for }G
\longrightarrow
\text{optimize approximants}.
\]

The new programme has the form

\[
\boxed{
\text{identify the minimal motive carrying }G
\longrightarrow
\text{classify its arithmetic realizations}
\longrightarrow
\text{choose the realization with maximal Diophantine capacity}.
}
\]

That is a fundamentally different search strategy.

The discovery of an independently constructed two-dimensional mixed motive with \(G\) as a period provides strong evidence that this is not an artificial reformulation: such a minimal host really exists.
